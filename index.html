About

A Fusion Like No Other

3 Kings is not just a band—it's a royal decree for unforgettable nights. Blending the raw power of live drums, ripping guitar solos, and masterclass DJ wizardry, this trio of elite performers turns every stage into a kingdom of sound.

With Ryan Patrick (guitar slayer from charting rock band OTHERWISE), Joe Conner (versatile industry drummer with thunderous groove), and DJ Chaney (the remix king with club-crushing instincts), 3 Kings delivers a sonic spectacle that fuses rock energy with electronic firepower.

Think renowned hits, both new and old, remixed into dance floor anthems—all performed with the explosive power of a rock show. Whether it's a high-end corporate event or a luxury private party, 3K knows how to make the ladies dance and the gents open the bar tab.
